Sri Lankan public buses are operated by SLTB (State) and private owners, forming an extensive nationwide network. Expressway buses operate on highways connecting Colombo to Galle, Matara, Kandy, and Jaffna, offering fast, air-conditioned journeys. Local intercity buses are cheap but can be crowded with frequent stops. Ticket prices on long-distance routes range from $1 to $5 USD equivalent. Express buses depart regularly from major bus stands like Pettah Central Bus Stand in Colombo.

Public bus transport is the most extensive and economical way to travel around Sri Lanka, reaching almost every city, town, and rural village. The bus system is divided into state-operated SLTB (red buses) and privately-owned buses.

Types of buses available:
- Normal (Non-AC) Buses: Highly frequent and extremely cheap (usually $0.50-$2 per journey). They can be crowded, lack air conditioning, and play loud music, but offer an authentic local experience.
- Intercity Express AC Buses: Smaller white microbuses that operate on long-distance routes (e.g. Colombo to Kandy, Galle, or Jaffna). They feature air conditioning, guaranteed seating, fewer stops, and faster travel times at slightly higher fares ($2-$6).
- Highway Express Buses: Run on the Southern Expressway (E01) connecting Colombo (Makumbura/Maharagama) to Galle, Matara, and Hambantota, reducing travel time to around 1.5 to 2 hours.

Bus tickets are purchased on board from the bus conductor. Major bus terminals in Colombo include Bastian Mawatha for private intercity buses and Pettah Central Bus Stand for SLTB buses.

Domestic air travel in Sri Lanka offers fast, scenic connections between major regional hubs, bypassing long overland road journeys. The primary domestic carriers are Cinnamon Air and SriLankan Air Taxi (seaplanes and light aircraft).

Key domestic air routes and water aerodromes include:
- Colombo (Bandaranaike International Airport / Ratmalana Airport) to Kandy (Polgolla Reservoir), Castlereagh Reservoir (Nuwara Eliya), Sigiriya, Trincomalee (China Bay), Batticaloa, and Koggala (Galle/Mirissa).
- Scenic amphibian seaplanes land directly on inland lakes, lagoons, and reservoirs, cutting travel time from Colombo to Kandy or Sigiriya down to just 30-45 minutes compared to 3-4 hours by road.

Baggage allowances on domestic flights are strict (typically 15-20 kg per passenger). Fares range from $150 to $300 one-way per person. Domestic flights are particularly favored by luxury travelers and time-conscious visitors seeking high-altitude aerial views of Sri Lanka's coastlines, forests, and tea plantations.

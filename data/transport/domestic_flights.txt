Cinnamon Air and SriLankan Air Taxi operate domestic flights and floatplane charters connecting Colombo (Bandaranaike International Airport or Ratmalana) to destinations like Kandy (Polgolla Reservoir), Sigiriya, Castlereagh, Dikwella, Koggala, and Trincomalee. Flight times are short (30-45 minutes), saving hours of road travel. Prices range from $150 to $300 USD per seat one-way. Baggage limits are strictly enforced (typically 15-20 kg).

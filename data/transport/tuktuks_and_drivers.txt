Tuk-tuks (three-wheelers) are ideal for short city trips and local sightseeing. Ride-hailing apps like PickMe and Uber operate in Colombo, Kandy, Galle, and major towns, providing metered pricing. In rural areas, agree on a price before starting the trip. For multi-day itineraries, hiring a private driver with an air-conditioned car or van costs roughly $50-$80 USD per day including fuel, toll fees, and driver accommodation. Private drivers provide convenience for complex routes across the hill country and cultural triangle.

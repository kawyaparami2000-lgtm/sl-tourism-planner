Three-wheelers (commonly known as tuk-tuks) and private cars with dedicated tourist drivers are the two most flexible modes of road travel in Sri Lanka.

Tuk-tuks:
Ideal for short distances within towns, beach hopping, and local sightseeing. In major urban centers like Colombo, Kandy, and Galle, ride-hailing apps such as PickMe and Uber offer metered tuk-tuk rides at upfront transparent prices. In smaller towns without app coverage, travelers should negotiate the fare before getting in or ensure the driver uses a meter ($0.30-$0.50 per kilometer). Self-drive tuk-tuk rentals are also popular among adventurous travelers holding an international driving permit endorsed by the Automobile Association of Ceylon (AAC).

Private Tourist Drivers:
Hiring a private air-conditioned car or van with an English-speaking driver is the preferred choice for families, couples, and small groups wanting stress-free, customized island tours. Rates range between $50 and $85 per day, which typically covers fuel, driver accommodation, insurance, and highway tolls. Private drivers provide flexibility, local knowledge, and door-to-door comfort.

Train travel in Sri Lanka is world-renowned for its scenic beauty, affordability, and cultural charm. The most famous route is the Main Line journey from Colombo through Kandy, Hatton, Nanu Oya (Nuwara Eliya), and Ella to Badulla. The segment between Kandy and Ella is widely considered one of the most picturesque railway journeys in the world, passing emerald tea plantations, cloud forests, and mountain bridges.

Train travel classes include:
- 1st Class Observation Car / Air-Conditioned Saloon: Reserved seating with large windows or AC. Booking opens 30 days in advance and sells out quickly.
- 2nd Class Reserved & Unreserved: Comfortable padded seats; unreserved tickets can be bought at the station on the day of travel.
- 3rd Class Reserved & Unreserved: Economical bench seating, popular among budget backpackers.

Express trains like the Ella Odyssey and Podi Menike run daily. Advance reservations can be made online via the Sri Lanka Railways portal or authorized ticketing agents. Coastal trains connecting Colombo, Bentota, Hikkaduwa, Galle, and Matara run along the ocean coastline.

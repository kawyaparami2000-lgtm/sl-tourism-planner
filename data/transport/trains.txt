Train travel in Sri Lanka is scenic and budget-friendly. The main line connecting Colombo, Kandy, Nuwara Eliya (Nanu Oya), and Ella is world-famous. Reservation for 1st class (air-conditioned) and 2nd class reserved seats opens 30 days prior to travel through Sri Lanka Railways or authorized booking agents. 3rd class unreserved tickets can be bought on the day of travel at stations. The coastal train line connects Colombo to Galle and Matara along the palm-lined shoreline. Expect minor delays during peak monsoon seasons.

Daily travel expenses in Sri Lanka:
- Backpacker Budget: $25 - $40 USD per day (hostels/homestays, public buses/trains, local rice & curry).
- Mid-range Budget: $60 - $120 USD per day (3-star hotels/boutique guesthouses, private driver or expressway buses, casual dining).
- Luxury Budget: $250 - $600+ USD per day (5-star resorts/villas, private chauffeur car, fine dining, private guided tours).
Major site entry fees: Sigiriya ($36 USD), Kandy Temple of Tooth ($10 USD), Yala Park ($30 USD). Local SIM cards with 30GB data cost ~$8 USD at Colombo Airport.

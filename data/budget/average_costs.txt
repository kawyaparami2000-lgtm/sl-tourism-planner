Planning a travel budget for Sri Lanka depends on choice of accommodation, transportation, dining, and activity preferences. Below is an overview of daily expenses across three main budget tiers:

Budget Tier ($25 - $45 USD per day per person):
- Stay: Hostels or local homestays ($8 - $20/night).
- Food: Local rice & curry plates, street food, hoppers ($2 - $5/meal).
- Transport: Public buses and 2nd/3rd class trains ($1 - $4/day).
- Activities: Free beach visits, low-cost temple visits, local walks.

Mid-Range Tier ($60 - $140 USD per day per person):
- Stay: Boutique guesthouses, 3-star hotels, or beach resorts ($45 - $90/night).
- Food: Casual restaurants, beach cafes, tourist eateries ($8 - $18/meal).
- Transport: Combination of 1st/2nd class trains, tuk-tuks, and occasional private driver transfers ($15 - $35/day).
- Activities: Wildlife safaris ($50 - $75), Sigiriya entry ($36), whale watching ($40 - $60).

Luxury Tier ($250 - $800+ USD per day per person):
- Stay: 5-star luxury resorts, tea planter bungalows, private villas ($250 - $700+/night).
- Food: Fine dining at high-end hotel restaurants, gourmet seafood ($35 - $80/meal).
- Transport: Dedicated private driver in AC vehicle ($60 - $85/day) or domestic seaplane flights.
- Activities: Private naturalist safaris, exclusive spa packages, private boat charters.

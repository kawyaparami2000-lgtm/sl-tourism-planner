Yala National Park, located in Sri Lanka's southeastern region, is the country's most famous wildlife safari destination. It boasts one of the highest leopard densities in the world (specifically Panthera pardus kotiya). Visitors on 4x4 jeep safaris can also encounter Asian elephants, sloth bears, spotted deer, wild boars, crocodiles, and over 200 bird species. Yala Block 1 is the primary safari zone. The park is closed annually for maintenance during the dry month of September. Tissamaharama serves as the main gateway city with safari lodge accommodation.

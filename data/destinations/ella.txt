Ella is a small mountain village in the central highlands of Sri Lanka, famous for its breathtaking mountain panoramas, tea estates, and hiking trails. Key attractions include Little Adam's Peak, Ella Rock, and the iconic Nine Arches Bridge, a architectural marvel where trains pass through scenic greenery. The train journey from Kandy to Ella is widely considered one of the most beautiful train rides in the world, winding through high-altitude cloud forests and tea plantations. Ella enjoys cool daytime temperatures around 20-25°C and chilly evenings. Accommodation ranges from backpacker hostels to luxury eco-lodges with valley views.

Ella is a small mountain village nestled in the Badulla district of Sri Lanka's central highlands, surrounded by misty cloud forests, tea estates, and dramatic mountain gaps. Situated at an elevation of roughly 1,000 meters, Ella boasts a cool climate and a relaxed, bohemian traveler atmosphere.

The area is a paradise for hikers and nature enthusiasts. Famous hiking spots include Little Adam's Peak, an easy 1-hour walk offering 360-degree valley views, and Ella Rock, a more challenging 3-to-4-hour trek through eucalyptus forests and tea gardens. The Nine Arch Bridge, an engineering marvel from the British colonial era constructed entirely of stone and brick without steel, is a popular spot to watch passing blue passenger trains.

Ella also features Ravana Falls and the ancient Ravana Cave associated with the Ramayana epic. The town main street is vibrant with backpacker cafes, organic restaurants, and live music venues. Most visitors stay 2 to 4 days, using Ella as a base for hiking, zip-lining at Flying Ravana, and visiting tea processing factories.

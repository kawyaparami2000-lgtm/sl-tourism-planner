Galle is a historical city on Sri Lanka's southwestern coast, famous for Galle Fort, a UNESCO World Heritage site built by the Portuguese in the 16th century and fortified by the Dutch in the 17th century. Inside the fort walls, narrow cobblestone streets are lined with Dutch colonial villas, boutique shops, art galleries, cafes, and historic ramparts overlooking the Indian Ocean. Galle is an ideal coastal base for exploring nearby southern beaches such as Unawatuna, Thalpe, and Mirissa. Galle Fort enjoys pleasant coastal weather, though monsoon rainfall can occur during the Southwest Monsoon (May to September). Travel time from Colombo is approximately 1.5 to 2 hours via the Southern Expressway.

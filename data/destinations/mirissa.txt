Mirissa is a popular coastal town on Sri Lanka's southern shore, famous for its golden sandy crescent beach, vibrant beach bars, coconut palm hills, and world-class marine wildlife experiences. It attracts surfers, backpackers, and couples seeking coastal relaxation.

Mirissa is renowned globally as one of the best locations for blue whale watching. Boat tours run daily from Mirissa harbor during the peak season from November to April, offering high probabilities of spotting blue whales, sperm whales, and pods of spinner dolphins. Coconut Tree Hill, a natural red-dirt promontory lined with towering palm trees jutting into the sea, is Mirissa's most famous photography spot.

Secret Beach provides a quiet, sheltered cove ideal for snorkeling among sea turtles and coral reefs. Nearby Weligama Bay offers shallow, gentle waves perfect for beginner surf lessons. In the evenings, Mirissa Beach transforms into a lively dining stretch with candlelit seafood display tables set right on the sand.

Mirissa is a popular coastal town on Sri Lanka's south coast, famous for blue whale watching excursions, surfing, coconut palm hills, and vibrant beach cafes. Whale watching season runs from November to April, during which blue whales, bryde's whales, and dolphins are frequently spotted offshore. Coconut Tree Hill and Secret Beach are top Instagrammable spots. Mirissa offers clear warm waters for swimming, snorkeling, and beginner surfing at Mirissa Point. Nightlife centers around beachfront seafood barbecues and acoustic beach bars.

Sigiriya, also known as the Lion Rock Fortress, is an ancient rock fortress and UNESCO World Heritage site located in the Central Province of Sri Lanka. Rising nearly 200 meters above the surrounding jungle, King Kasyapa built his palace atop this granite peak in the 5th century AD. Highlights include the ancient water gardens, world-renowned frescoes of celestial maidens, the giant Lion's Paws gateway, and panoramic summit views. Nearby Pidurangala Rock offers an alternative climb with incredible sunset views of Sigiriya itself. Sigiriya is best visited early in the morning to avoid heat and crowds. It lies within the Cultural Triangle along with Dambulla Cave Temple.

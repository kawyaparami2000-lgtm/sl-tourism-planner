Nuwara Eliya, often called 'Little England', is a hill country station known for its cool climate, colonial-era architecture, manicured golf courses, and vast Ceylon tea plantations. Situated at an elevation of 1,868 meters, temperatures drop to 10-18°C. Major sights include Gregory Lake, Hakgala Botanical Garden, Pedro Tea Estate, and Horton Plains National Park (home to World's End precipice and Baker's Falls). Visitors should pack warm clothing for chilly evenings. Nuwara Eliya is accessible by train via Nanu Oya station or by road from Kandy or Ella.

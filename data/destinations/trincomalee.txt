Trincomalee is a coastal city on the northeastern coast of Sri Lanka, renowned for its pristine white-sand beaches like Nilaveli and Uppuveli, natural deep-water harbor, and historical sites. Pigeon Island National Park off Nilaveli beach offers world-class snorkeling and diving among blacktip reef sharks and sea turtles. Historical landmarks include Koneswaram Temple perched on Swami Rock cliff, Fort Frederick, and Kanniya Hot Springs. Best weather on the east coast occurs from May to September during the Southwest Monsoon, making Trincomalee an ideal summer beach destination.

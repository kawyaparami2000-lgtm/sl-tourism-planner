Trincomalee, situated on the northeastern coast of Sri Lanka, is famous for possessing one of the world's finest natural deep-water harbors, pristine white sand beaches, and deep Hindu cultural heritage. Key beach resorts include Nilaveli and Uppuveli.

Pigeon Island National Park, located just 1 km off the coast of Nilaveli, is Sri Lanka's premier marine sanctuary, offering crystal-clear snorkeling with blacktip reef sharks, hawksbill turtles, and vibrant coral formations. Koneswaram Temple, perched atop Swami Rock cliff overlooking the sea inside Fort Frederick, is an ancient, revered Hindu pilgrimage site dedicated to Lord Shiva.

Trincomalee enjoys its dry summer season from May to September, making it the ideal east coast alternative when the southwest monsoon rains affect southern beaches. Blue whales and sperm whales can also be spotted off Trincomalee's deep continental shelf between March and August.

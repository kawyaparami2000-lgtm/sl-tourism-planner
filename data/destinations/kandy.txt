Kandy is the cultural capital of Sri Lanka, located in the central hill country surrounded by lush mountain ranges and tea plantations. The city centers around the scenic Kandy Lake and is world-famous for housing the Temple of the Sacred Tooth Relic (Sri Dalada Maligawa), one of Buddhism's most revered shrines.

Visitors to Kandy can explore the Royal Botanical Gardens in Peradeniya, which features over 4,000 species of tropical plants, palm avenues, and vibrant orchid collections. Kandy's climate is milder than the coastal plains, with average temperatures ranging between 20°C and 28°C. The annual Esala Perahera festival, held in July or August, features grand nighttime processions with traditional dancers, drummers, and decorated elephants.

Travelers usually allocate 2 to 3 days in Kandy to visit historical temples such as Lankatilaka and Gadaladeniya, enjoy traditional Kandyan dance performances, and sample local teas at nearby estates. Kandy also serves as the starting point for the famous scenic train journey through the highlands to Ella and Nuwara Eliya.

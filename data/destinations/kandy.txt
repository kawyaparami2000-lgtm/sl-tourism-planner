Kandy is the cultural capital of Sri Lanka, situated in the central hills surrounded by lush mountains and tea plantations. It is home to the sacred Temple of the Tooth Relic (Sri Dalada Maligawa), one of the most venerated Buddhist shrines in the world. Visitors can explore Kandy Lake, watch traditional Kandyan dance performances, and visit the Royal Botanical Gardens at Peradeniya, which features over 4,000 species of plants. Kandy experiences a cooler hill country climate compared to coastal areas. Budget travelers can stay in guesthouses around the lake, while midrange and luxury boutique hotels offer scenic hill views. Travel time from Colombo is approximately 3.5 hours by scenic train or 3 hours by highway bus.

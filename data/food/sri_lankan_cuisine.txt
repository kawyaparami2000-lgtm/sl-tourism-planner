Sri Lankan food is rich in spices, herbs, and coconut milk. Staples include Rice and Curry featuring dhal (lentil curry), fish or chicken curry, and sambols like Pol Sambol (spiced grated coconut). Popular street foods include Kottu Roti (chopped flatbread stir-fried with vegetables and meat), Hoppers (appa - bowl-shaped rice flour pancakes), String Hoppers (idiyappam), and fresh seafood dishes like Jaffna crab curry. Tropical fruits include king coconut water, mangosteen, rambutan, and woodapple juice. Average meal price ranges from $2-$5 USD at local eateries.

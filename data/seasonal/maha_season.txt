The Maha Monsoon season (North-East Monsoon) affects Sri Lanka from October/November through February. Rain-bearing winds blow from the Bay of Bengal, bringing precipitation to the Northern and Eastern provinces as well as parts of the central hill country.

Impact on travel regions:
- North & East Coast: Trincomalee, Batticaloa, Pasikudah, Arugam Bay, and Jaffna experience their main rainy season, leading to rough seas and limited water sports activities.
- Southwest Coast Peak Season: Conversely, the Southwestern and Southern coasts (Colombo, Bentota, Galle, Hikkaduwa, Unawatuna, Mirissa, Tangalle) and the Western hill country enjoy their peak dry season from December to April. Skies are clear, waters are calm, and conditions are ideal for beach holidays, whale watching, and diving.

Travel Advice for December-April:
This is the island's peak international tourist season. Travelers should focus their itineraries on the South-West beaches, Galle Fort, Kandy, Nuwara Eliya, and Yala National Park. Booking accommodation and train tickets weeks in advance is essential during the December-January holiday surge.

The Maha Monsoon (Northeast Monsoon) affects the northern and eastern regions from October to January, bringing heavy rain to Trincomalee, Batticaloa, and Jaffna. Meanwhile, the southern, western, and hill country regions experience dry, sunny, and favorable weather from November to April, making this the peak tourist season for beach holidays in Galle, Mirissa, and hiking in Ella. Inter-monsoonal thunder showers can occur island-wide in October and November.

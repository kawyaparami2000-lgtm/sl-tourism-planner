The Yala Monsoon (Southwest Monsoon) brings rainfall to Sri Lanka's south, west, and central hill regions from May to September. During this period, sea conditions on southwestern beaches (Galle, Hikkaduwa, Mirissa, Bentota) can be rough with high waves, making eastern and northern destinations (Trincomalee, Arugam Bay, Pasikuda, Jaffna) the ideal choice for sunshine and calm waters. Temperature remains warm year-round along coasts (27-31°C).

The Yala Monsoon season (South-West Monsoon) affects Sri Lanka from May through September. During this period, rain-bearing winds blow from the Indian Ocean, bringing heavy rainfall, sea swells, and stormy weather primarily to the South-West region of the island.

Impact on travel regions:
- Southwestern Coasts & Hills: Colombo, Bentota, Hikkaduwa, Galle, Mirissa, and Nuwara Eliya experience frequent rain showers and choppy ocean conditions. Ocean swimming along southern beaches can be dangerous due to strong rip currents.
- Cultural Triangle & East Coast Advantage: Interestingly, while the southwest receives monsoon rain, the Northern, Eastern, and Cultural Triangle regions (Trincomalee, Pasikudah, Arugam Bay, Sigiriya, Dambulla, Polonnaruwa, Jaffna) remain dry, sunny, and warm.

Travel Advice for May-September:
Travelers visiting Sri Lanka between May and September are strongly advised to head to the East Coast (Trincomalee, Pasikudah, Arugam Bay for world-class surfing) and the Cultural Triangle, where weather conditions are sunny and optimal for outdoor activities and beach holidays.

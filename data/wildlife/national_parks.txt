Sri Lanka is a global biodiversity hotspot. Top national parks include: Yala National Park (leopards and bears), Udawalawe National Park (guaranteed elephant herds), Wilpattu National Park (largest park with dense forest lakes and leopards), Minneriya National Park (famous for 'The Gathering' of hundreds of wild elephants around Minneriya Tank from July to September), and Horton Plains National Park (montane cloud forest hiking). Safari entry fees average $25-$35 USD per adult plus jeep rental ($35-$50).

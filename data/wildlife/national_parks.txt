Sri Lanka is recognized as one of Asia's top biodiversity hotspots, offering extraordinary wildlife viewing across diverse terrestrial national parks and marine sanctuaries.

Key Wildlife Parks:
- Yala National Park: Famous for having the world's highest density of Sri Lankan leopards, along with elephants, sloth bears, and crocodiles.
- Udawalawe National Park: Located in the south-central dry zone, Udawalawe guarantees elephant sightings year-round. Its reservoir and grasslands support over 500 wild Asian elephants. The Udawalawe Elephant Transit Home rehabilitates orphaned elephant calves.
- Minneriya & Kaudulla National Parks: Famous for "The Elephant Gathering," a phenomenon taking place from July to October where up to 300 wild elephants congregate around Minneriya reservoir tank to feed on fresh grass.
- Wilpattu National Park: Sri Lanka's largest and oldest national park, known for its natural sand-rimmed water basins (villus), dense forests, leopards, and sloth bears in a peaceful, uncrowded setting.
- Horton Plains National Park: High-altitude montane cloud forest ideal for walking safaris, endemic birdwatching, and observing sambar deer.

Mid-range accommodation in Sri Lanka provides an exceptional balance of comfort, charm, style, and value, priced between $45 and $110 per night.

Types of mid-range properties:
- Boutique Hotels & Colonial Villas: Restored Dutch and British colonial homes converted into stylish boutique hotels featuring high ceilings, courtyard gardens, four-poster beds, and swimming pools (especially inside Galle Fort, Kandy, and Nuwara Eliya).
- Beach Resorts & Eco Lodges: Property options along the West, South, and East coasts (Bentota, Hikkaduwa, Tangalle, Trincomalee) offering oceanfront rooms, swimming pools, on-site restaurants, and spa facilities. In wildlife hubs like Habarana and Sigiriya, mid-range eco lodges offer treehouses and chalets set within natural jungle environments.

Amenities standard in mid-range stays include air conditioning, private balconies, swimming pools, complimentary buffet or plated breakfasts, tea/coffee makers, and dedicated tour booking desks. Mid-range stays are ideal for couples, families, and leisure travelers seeking high comfort without luxury price tags.

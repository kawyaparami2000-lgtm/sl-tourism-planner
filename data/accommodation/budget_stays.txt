Budget accommodation options in Sri Lanka are abundant, clean, and welcoming, catering to backpackers, solo travelers, and budget-conscious tourists.

Types of budget stays:
- Hostels & Dormitories: Located in popular tourist spots like Ella, Mirissa, Arugam Bay, Kandy, and Colombo. Prices range from $6 to $15 per night for a dorm bed with air conditioning or fan, lockers, and shared social areas. Major hostel chains include Hosteller, Mad Monkey, and regional eco-hostels.
- Family Homestays & Guesthouses: Family-run guesthouses offer private rooms with attached bathrooms for $15 to $30 per night. Staying at a homestay allows travelers to enjoy home-cooked Sri Lankan breakfasts (string hoppers, curry, fresh fruit) and personalized advice from hospitable local hosts.
- Beach Huts & Eco Cabanas: Found along surf beaches like Arugam Bay, Hiriketiya, and Tangalle, offering rustic open-air living near the ocean.

Basic amenities generally include free Wi-Fi, mosquito nets, hot water showers, and optional breakfast. Booking via platforms like Hostelworld, Booking.com, or Agoda is recommended during peak tourist season.

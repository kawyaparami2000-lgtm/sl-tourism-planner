Budget accommodation in Sri Lanka includes backpacker hostels, family-run guesthouses, and homestays. Homestays often include home-cooked traditional breakfasts and warm local hospitality. Prices range from $10 to $25 USD per night for clean private rooms or dorm beds. Popular hubs for budget stays are Ella, Mirissa, Arugam Bay, and Kandy. Booking via platforms like Hostelworld, Booking.com, or Agoda is standard.

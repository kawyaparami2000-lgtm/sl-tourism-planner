Sri Lanka's luxury hospitality sector is world-renowned, combining architectural brilliance, personalized butler service, gourmet culinary experiences, and breathtaking natural settings. Prices range from $250 to $1,000+ per night.

Iconic luxury retreats include:
- Tea Country Bungalows: Ceylon Tea Trails, a collection of restored 19th-century tea planter bungalows in the Hatton region overlooking Castlereagh Lake, offers all-inclusive butler service, high tea, and scenic helicopter landings.
- Wildlife Glamping Lodges: Wild Coast Tented Lodge adjacent to Yala National Park features futuristic canvas-clad arched pavilions, plunge pools, and expert naturalist-guided safaris.
- Tropical Modernist Resorts: Designed by master architect Geoffrey Bawa, properties like Heritance Kandalama (built into a cliffside overgrown with jungle near Sigiriya) and Anantara Peace Haven Tangalle Resort set benchmarks in architectural design and coastal luxury.
- Galle Fort Grand Hotels: Amangalla inside Galle Fort delivers historic colonial grandeur, fine dining, and holistic Ayurvedic spa treatments.

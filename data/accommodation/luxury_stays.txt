Luxury resorts in Sri Lanka range from $150 to $600+ USD per night. Iconic properties include Geoffrey Bawa architect-designed hotels (Heritance Kandalama, Jetwing Lighthouse), high-end safari glamping lodges in Yala (Wild Coast Tented Lodge), luxury tea bungalow estates in Hatton (Tea Trails), and 5-star beach hideaways in Tangalle and Pasikuda. Luxury stays feature fine dining, private butler service, and spa treatments.

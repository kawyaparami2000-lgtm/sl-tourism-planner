All international travelers visiting Sri Lanka for tourism purposes require an Electronic Travel Authorization (ETA) or tourist visa prior to or upon arrival.

Key Visa Guidelines:
- Official Portal: Tourists should apply online through the official government portal (srilankaevisa.lk or the official ETA website). Avoid unauthorized third-party agencies that charge exorbitant service fees.
- Visa Validity: Standard tourist visas allow entry for up to 30 days, with options for single, double, or multiple entries depending on nationality. Visas can be extended online or at the Department of Immigration & Emigration in Battaramulla, Colombo, up to a total stay of 6 months.
- Passport Validity: Passports must be valid for at least 6 months beyond the intended date of departure from Sri Lanka, with at least two blank pages.
- Visa on Arrival (VOA): Available at Bandaranaike International Airport (BIA) for select nationalities, though applying online beforehand is recommended to prevent airport queue delays.
- Fee Structure: Standard tourist ETA fees range between $20 and $50 depending on SAARC vs non-SAARC nationality. Exemptions or free visa schemes may apply for specific tourist source countries depending on active government tourism promotional campaigns.

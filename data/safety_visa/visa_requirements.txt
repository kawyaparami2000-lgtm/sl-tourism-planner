Most foreign visitors require an Electronic Travel Authorization (ETA) or eVisa prior to entering Sri Lanka, obtainable online via official government portals. Tourist visas are typically valid for 30 days with double entry options, extendable up to 6 months at the Department of Immigration in Colombo. Passports must have at least 6 months validity remaining from the date of arrival. Visa fees range from $20 to $50 USD depending on nationality, with some countries granted visa fee waivers.

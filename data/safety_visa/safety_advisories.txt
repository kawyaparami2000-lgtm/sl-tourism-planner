Sri Lanka is generally a safe, welcoming travel destination. Basic precautions: avoid unmetered tuk-tuks, use trusted ride apps like PickMe or Uber in urban areas, and exercise care when swimming in ocean currents during monsoon seasons. Modest dress covering shoulders and knees is mandatory when entering Buddhist temples (remove shoes and hats). Tattoos of Buddha must be covered as public display can cause offense. Emergency police hotline is 119; Tourist Police hotline is 1912.

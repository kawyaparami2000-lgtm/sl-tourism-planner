Sri Lanka is generally a very safe and welcoming destination for international travelers, including solo female travelers and families. Crime rates targeting tourists are low, but standard travel precautions are advised.

Safety Recommendations:
- Solo Traveler Advice: Exercise routine precautions when traveling at night in unlit areas. Use reliable ride-hailing apps (PickMe/Uber) for nighttime transportation rather than flagging down unregistered vehicles. Keep emergency contacts handy.
- Cultural Etiquette: Dress modestly when visiting Buddhist and Hindu temples (knees and shoulders must be covered, hats and shoes removed). Never pose with your back turned directly towards a Buddha statue for photographs, as this is considered respectful breach.
- Ocean & Swimming Safety: Pay attention to beach flag warning systems along coastal areas. Strong undertows and rip currents occur during monsoon months along the South-West and East coasts.
- Health & Tap Water: Drink bottled or boiled water; avoid drinking untreated tap water. Use mosquito repellent containing DEET to guard against dengue fever, particularly during rainy seasons. Emergency tourist police hotlines (1912) operate 24/7 across major tourist hubs.

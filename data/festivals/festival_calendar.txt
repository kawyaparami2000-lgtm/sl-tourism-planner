Sri Lanka boasts a vibrant cultural calendar rich with religious and cultural festivals celebrated by Buddhist, Hindu, Muslim, and Christian communities throughout the year.

Major Annual Festivals:
- Duruthu Perahera (January): Held at Kelaniya Raja Maha Vihara near Colombo, commemorating the Buddha's first visit to Sri Lanka with traditional drummers, flame dancers, and whip-crackers.
- Sinhala & Tamil New Year (Aluth Avurudda - April): A major national holiday marking the sun's transit into Aries. Families prepare sweetmeats (oil cakes/kavum, kokis), wear traditional clothes, and participate in traditional village games.
- Vesak Full Moon Poya (May): The most sacred Buddhist festival celebrating the birth, enlightenment, and passing of the Buddha. Towns across the island are illuminated with giant hand-crafted paper lanterns (Vesak kudu) and free food stalls (dansalas).
- Kandy Esala Perahera (July/August): Sri Lanka's grandest ten-day pageant featuring the Sacred Tooth Relic casket carried atop a majestic tusker elephant accompanied by fire-dancers and Kandyan percussionists.
- Nallur Kovil Festival (August/September): A 25-day Hindu festival in Jaffna featuring vibrant chariot processions and religious devotion.

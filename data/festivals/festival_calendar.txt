Sri Lanka celebrates vibrant religious and cultural festivals. Esala Perahera in Kandy (July/August) features grand processions of decorated elephants, drummers, and fire dancers honoring the Sacred Tooth Relic. Sinhala and Tamil New Year (Aluth Avurudda) in mid-April marks the harvest with traditional games and sweets. Vesak Poya in May honors Buddha's birth with illuminated bamboo lanterns and free food stalls (dansalas) across towns. Vel Festival in Colombo features colorful Hindu chariot processions.
